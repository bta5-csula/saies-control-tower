What activities generate emissions?

Each bar shows CO2e from one activity category and its share of total. Overstock is typically the largest source — unsold inventory generates carbon even when nothing ships.

Which organizations emit the most?

CO2e attributed to each sales organization. A large gap between the highest and lowest emitters suggests room to improve operations in higher-emitting locations.

How are emissions classified by scope?

Scope 3 (value chain — suppliers, shipping, customers) is usually the largest share and the hardest to reduce. Focus here for the biggest impact.

How did emissions change over time?

Total CO2e per simulation period. Rising emissions over time may indicate growing overstock or increased shipping activity.
